Pseudo elements

A paragraph is a group of related sentences that develop a single, central idea or topic, acting as a unit of thought in writing. It typically consists of a topic sentence stating the main point, supporting sentences providing details, and a concluding sentence that summarizes or transitions. Key elements of a paragraph include: Topic Sentence: Presents the main idea or "controlling idea" of the paragraph. Supporting Sentences: Develop the topic with facts, examples, or evidence, often using transition words for coherence. Concluding Sentence: Summarizes the point and provides closure or a bridge to the next paragraph.
